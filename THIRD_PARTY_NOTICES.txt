2. GraphBot

Source: https://github.com/KroSheChKa/GraphBot
Used in: botmain/src/gwbot/perception/window_capture.py
Files: logic adapted from core/game_capture.py and core/window_capture.py
Modifications: reduced to the off-screen PrintWindow client capture; window
lookup prefers an exact title match and rejects zero-sized client areas, so a
terminal or editor window that contains "Graphwar" in its title is not picked;
no browser UI, planner, or config files are included.
